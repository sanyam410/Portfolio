A little about me

Curiosity first.
Evidence always.

My approach to data begins with understanding the problem, examining the evidence, and choosing methods that make the results meaningful. I value clear reasoning over assumptions and conclusions that the data can actually support.

My projects range from SQL-based data warehousing and exploratory film analysis to Power BI dashboards, regression model comparison, and a plot-based movie recommender. Each project gives me an opportunity to investigate a problem, apply a method, and document the results.
